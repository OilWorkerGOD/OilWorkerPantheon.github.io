// CONCEPT — DAY ZERO

It Starts Here. Alone.

I got removed from the team I helped found. No drama. Just a door closing.

So I opened a new one. This is Red Project — a game about economies, rules, and emergent systems. No characters. No story. No 3D open world. Just systems that behave like real systems do: complex, fragile, and infinitely surprising.

Think Balatro meets Conway's Game of Life, set in ancient Greece. You are the legislator. The economy obeys your laws. Or doesn't.

Engine: Unity. Team size: 1. Timeline: whenever it's ready.
