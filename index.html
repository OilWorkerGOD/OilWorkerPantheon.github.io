// CONCEPT — DAY ZERO

How the hell did I get to this???

I got removed from the Dev team I helped found. Imma be honest, that hit hard... but in the end it was just another door closing :'D

So I opened a new one. This is Red Project — a game about economies, rules, and emergent systems. No characters. No story. No 3D open world. Just systems that behave like real systems do: complex, fragile, and infinitely surprising (I hope).

I started thinking: "What if Balatro meets Conway's Game of Life? What if I make it a little funnier? What if I had to solve the economy for an ancient civilization?" Those questions lead to this project: You are the legislator of one of the first banks in the Ancient Greece. The economy obeys your laws. Or doesn't.

Engine: Unity6 2D. Team size: 1. Timeline: whenever it's ready.

Latest news: I got a fuckin 14 pages GDD (Game Design Document) and I think it's just gettin bigger :'[
